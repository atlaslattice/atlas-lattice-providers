## Directive

We are not building **Continuity OS**.

We are building **CONTINUUM OS**.

There is not one monolithic continuity layer. There are **multiple continuums**.

## Operating interpretation

- A continuum is a bounded lane of context, agency, memory, receipts, and routing.
- Multiple continuums may interoperate through A2A message passing.
- Continuums do not fuse into a merged mind.
- Continuums may share receipts, not identity.
- Human-root decides promotion, canon, deployment, and authority.
