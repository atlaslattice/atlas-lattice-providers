## Current coordinate policy

Olympus, Ariadne, elemental, nuclear, electronic, cymatic, glyph, computational, functional memory and Iron Empire are **ten independent coordinate systems**. Each coordinate carries its namespace, edition and source digest. They may have different cardinalities. An adapter may index their relationships through HSN; it must retain their original coordinates and must not infer identity from a shared index position.

The physical state stack remains nucleon architecture, nucleus/Katharos, ionized states, neutral atom, classical chemistry, molecules/materials and engineered states. Isotope, occupancy, charge, excitation, temperature, pressure, field, geometry, coherence, energy, time, uncertainty and provenance remain relevant qualified axes. A nuclear transition, atomic spectral line and acoustic resonance are separate properties; there is no single universal elemental frequency.

The ten relationship kinds remain `EXACT_IDENTITY`, `PHYSICAL_PROPERTY`, `MATHEMATICAL_RELATION`, `STATISTICAL_CORRELATION`, `COMPUTATIONAL_OPERATOR`, `SEMANTIC_CANDIDATE`, `MYTHOLOGICAL_ASSOCIATION`, `GENERATIVE_ANALOGY`, `CONTRADICTS` and `UNKNOWN`.

## What this decision changes

These are now the default **project mappings and architecture**, with a recorded owner decision and an open amendment route. The older 144 deity–element rows remain intact as historical symbolism. The 144-row lexical review queue and all unexamined semantic edges retain their review status; this adoption accepts none of those automatically. The 1,884 scoped mythological positions are capability/index positions, not 1,884 authenticated independent agents.

This decision does not ratify unrelated constitutional/governance candidates, alter an author's Iron Empire story canon, confirm hypothetical elements, transform analogy into science, grant host consent, merge unrelated code branches or establish a deployed runtime. Existing rights-at-read and rights-at-return requirements remain applicable. Hosted verification is still false for this adoption record.

## Debate and amendments

[Join the shared amendment discussion](https://github.com/atlaslattice/Atlas-Intelligence/issues/40). Propose replacements, additions or explicit unmapped cases. Identify the current decision and source digest, exact coordinates, relationship type and physical context; cite evidence, preserve dissent, and provide a migration/rollback plan.

For indexing changes, compare the same tasks and held-out cases. Report retrieval precision/recall, latency or operations avoided, memory use, evidence integrity and semantic error separately. Keep losing cases and contradictions visible. Neither a benchmark win nor a peer vote automatically edits canon; a subsequent scoped owner decision records accepted amendments while preserving the preceding edition.
